The Hybrid Optical-Electronic Chip

The NRadix chip splits matrix multiplication across two physical domains: light handles the multiplications (all at once), electronics handles the additions.

Why This Matters
The optical chip does the MULTIPLY -- the expensive part -- all N2 multiplications happen at the speed of light, simultaneously, in a single clock cycle. Electronics does the ACCUMULATE -- the cheap part -- simple addition in adder trees.

The result: same throughput as a TPU, at a fraction of the power. Light does not dissipate heat the way transistor switching does, so the most power-hungry operation (multiply) becomes nearly free.
